SPONSORSHIP OPPORTUNITIES


We are pleased to invite you to sponsor the 34th IFIP/IEEE International Conference on Very Large Scale Integration SoC (VLSI-SoC 2026), which will take place in Limassol (Cyprus), 11-14 October 2026. This prestigious conference is a global forum for researchers, industry experts, and academics to engage in discussions on the latest trends in semiconductor design, System-on-Chip (SoC) technology, embedded systems, and beyond.

Organized by IFIP WG 10.5, the conference has a long-standing reputation for excellence and attracts professionals from both academia and industry.

Why Sponsor VLSI-SoC 2026?

1. Strategic Location & Business Gateway

  • Access to European & Middle Eastern Markets Cyprus serves as a gateway to business collaborations between Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa (MENA). Global semiconductor and electronics leaders frequently establish regional partnerships in these regions. Sponsoring the event provides a direct avenue for networking with influential stakeholders.
  • EU Membership & Business-Friendly Policies Cyprus offers one of the lowest corporate tax rates in the EU (12.5%) and a robust intellectual property (IP) regime, making it an attractive hub for R&D centers, technology investments, and partnerships.
  • Growing Tech Ecosystem The region is home to advanced research in AI hardware, embedded systems, and semiconductor technology, attracting high-tech R&D centers and multinational corporations.

2. Industry-Specific Benefits

  • Direct access to academic & industry talent: The conference provides a recruitment pipeline for top-tier VLSI and semiconductor engineers from leading institutions.
  • Brand visibility in semiconductor & embedded systems: Gain exposure to decision-makers from semiconductor, automotive tech, and embedded system firms.
  • Networking with key stakeholders: Previous VLSI-SoC conferences have attracted major industrial players from the EDA and semiconductor ecosystem.

3. Cyprus as an Attractive Conference Destination

  • Cost-effective yet high-quality venue: Compared to major hubs like Paris, London, or Milan, Cyprus offers a more affordable yet high-impact alternative, ensuring higher ROI for sponsors.
  • Proven conference host: Cyprus has a strong track record in successfully hosting tech and scientific events.
